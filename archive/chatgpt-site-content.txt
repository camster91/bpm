GOOD CHEMISTRY.

Here’s what the ingredients do, in plain language.

01
Control odour.
Magnesium hydroxide helps neutralise odour. Zinc ricinoleate helps trap it.

02
Absorb moisture.
Tapioca starch and kaolin help absorb moisture and give the cream a drier feel.

03
Keep it smooth.
Fractionated coconut oil gives the formula glide. Sunflower oil helps soften skin.

04
Hold it together.
Plant-based thickeners and olive-derived ingredients help keep the texture creamy and even.
