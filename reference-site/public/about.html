FROM THE FOUNDER

It started
in a kitchen.

I started making deodorants in my kitchen in 2020 because I could not find one that met the full standard I had in mind. It needed to perform well, use natural ingredients, come in better packaging, feel good on skin, and still be priced fairly.

I did not come to BPM from a chemistry background. My background is in technology and product development, and that shaped the way I built this brand: by being thoughtful about every requirement, clear about priorities, and careful with the details.

I believed there was room for something better, and that natural products should be accessible to more people. BPM grew from that belief.

Corey Goss
Founder and President

FOUR PILLARS

The standard
behind the tube.

01

Performance comes first.

It fights odour, goes on smoothly and absorbs quickly. Product development starts with what the product needs to do.

02

Potion matters.

Every ingredient is natural or naturally derived. If it doesn’t improve performance or skin feel, it doesn’t stay.

03

Packaging, responsibly chosen.

Eco-friendly inks. Kraft paper boxes. Thoughtful material choices that balance sustainability with real-world performance.

04

Price with purpose.

Fair. Transparent. No inflated margins hiding behind “natural.” As BPM grows, the aim is to lower prices.
