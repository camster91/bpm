THE SCENT STORY

NOTHING EXTRA. EVERYTHING INTENTIONAL.

Good chemistry.

Here’s what the ingredients do, in plain language.

01

Control odour.

Magnesium hydroxide helps neutralise odour. Zinc ricinoleate helps trap it.

02

Absorb moisture.

Tapioca starch and kaolin help absorb moisture and give the cream a drier feel.

03

Keep it smooth.

Fractionated coconut oil gives the formula glide. Sunflower oil helps soften skin.

04

Hold it together.

Plant-based thickeners and olive-derived ingredients help keep the texture creamy and even.

A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT BE WONDERING

Good questions.

How much should I use?+

Start with a pea-sized amount and massage it into your underarms. Adjust the amount if needed.

Is BPM baking soda free?+

Yes. Both Bergamot & Lime and Unscented are formulated without baking soda or waxes.

How long does a tube last?+

BPM estimates approximately 304 pea-sized applications per 76 g tube, or around five months for one person. Your use may vary.

Where do you ship?+

Canada and the United States. Single tubes ship for $1.50 in Canada; Side A / Side B ships free in Canada. U.S. shipping is free above $45 CAD, or $7 CAD otherwise.
